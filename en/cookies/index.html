Cookie Policy

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website may store on your device. Other technologies, such as browser local storage, can also store or access information.

How this version works

The website's own code at bernardoramirez.com does not include cookies, local storage, advertising tracking or visitor measurement tools. The language is selected using your browser settings or your choice in the page address, without storing a language cookie.

The updates form is part of this website. Google reCAPTCHA, which protects it from spam, loads only when you start using the form (by tapping or typing in it). Your browser then connects to Google, which may use cookies or similar technologies to tell people from bots, under its privacy policy. When you submit the form, your email is sent directly to Brevo. Direct email contact remains available without using the form.

The selected hosting service is GitHub Pages. Its security IP logs are distinct from cookies; this processing is explained in the Privacy Policy. The absence of cookies in the website's own code does not mean the hosting provider processes no personal data.

Changes and controls

If services that store or access information on your device are introduced, I will update this policy to explain their purposes, providers and duration. Where your consent is required, those technologies will not be activated before it is obtained, and you will be able to accept, reject or withdraw your choice easily.

You can manage data stored by websites through your browser settings. This does not replace any consent controls the website is legally required to provide.
